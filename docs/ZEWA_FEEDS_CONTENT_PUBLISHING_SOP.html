✅ The 7-Point Pre-Flight Checklist

Complete these 7 checks before clicking "Publish now":

  • Title & Excerpt: Title is under 180 chars; Excerpt is engaging and under 300 chars.
  • URL Slug: Slug is clean and lowercase with hyphens (e.g. protein-absorption-in-fish).
  • Desktop Cover Asset: High-resolution image selected from the Media Library with descriptive alt text.
  • Block Formatting: Headings (H2) have clear text and short anchor IDs for jump links.
  • Figure Images: All inline figures have alt text and appropriate aspect ratios (16:9 or 4:3).
  • Related Articles: Picked 1 or 2 related articles to boost reader retention and lower bounce rate.
  • Preview Inspected: Clicked "Preview" and checked layout on both desktop and mobile viewports.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting
Common questions from the Content Team answered:
Q1: Can I change the URL slug of an article after it has been published?

No, by design. Once an article is published, the slug is permanently locked. Changing a live slug breaks existing customer bookmarks, social media shares, and Google index rankings (causing 404 errors). If a slug change is ever mission-critical, an Admin must set up a 301 redirect.

Q2: What happens if I edit an article that is already live?

When you edit a live article and click "Save draft", your edits are saved as an unpublished draft overlay. Visitors on zewafeeds.com continue seeing the existing live version. The new edits only go live when you press "Publish now".

Q3: How do inline links work inside paragraphs?

Under any Paragraph block, click "+ Link a phrase". Enter the exact phrase in that paragraph (e.g., insect protein) and the target URL (e.g., /products/micro-pellets). The CMS automatically hyperlinks that phrase cleanly!

Q4: How do I remove an article from the live website?

Open the article and click "Unpublish" in the top bar. It immediately reverts to a Draft, disappears from the blog index and Knowledge Hub, and returns to your draft list.
